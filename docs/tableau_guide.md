# Tableau Dashboard Guide

**Workbook:** `tableau/European_Beer_Trade_Dashboard.twbx`

**Project:** European Beer Trade Dynamics (2019–2025)

## Overview

This Tableau workbook presents an analysis of European beer trade dynamics between 2019 and 2025.

The analysis integrates official Eurostat beer trade statistics with World Bank population and GDP-per-capita indicators.

The workbook contains **three dashboards and six worksheets**, designed to examine:

1. Overall EU-27 beer export trends and changes in country export shares.
2. Geographic trade dynamics and differences between intra-EU and extra-EU export growth.
3. Beer export intensity per capita and its relationship with economic indicators.

The objective is to provide descriptive evidence that can support comparative market analysis, while clearly distinguishing observed patterns from causal explanations.

## Data Sources

| Source | File | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `beer_trade_kpis` | `data/processed/beer_trade_kpis.csv` | Country-year KPIs: export share, export volume, export growth, exports per capita and GDP per capita |

Both datasets are generated by the reproducible Python pipeline:

    uv run python run_pipeline.py

The underlying raw data are preserved in `data/raw/`.

Additional information about sources, collection methods and data provenance is available in `docs/data_sources.md`.

## Dashboard 01 — EU Beer Trade Overview

**Purpose:** Understand the evolution of European beer exports and identify the countries gaining or losing relative export share.

### Worksheet 1 — EU-27 Beer Export Volume (2019–2025)

**Visualization:** Line chart.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_kpis`.

**Configuration:**
- Columns: `year`
- Rows: `SUM(export_kg)`
- Time period: 2019–2025
- Geographic coverage: EU-27

The chart shows the evolution of total reported beer export volume across the EU-27 countries.

**Main observation:**

Aggregate reported export volume decreased from approximately 9.14 billion kg in 2019 to 7.78 billion kg in 2025, representing a decline of approximately 14.9%.

This indicator should be interpreted as the sum of reported country exports, not as a measure of beer production or consumption.

### Worksheet 2 — Export Share Winners & Losers

**Visualization:** Diverging horizontal bar chart.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_kpis`.

**Configuration:**
- Rows: Country
- Columns: Export share change in percentage points
- Comparison: 2019 versus 2025
- Countries sorted by export share change

**Calculated field: Export Share Change (pp)**

Conceptually:

    (Export Share 2025 - Export Share 2019) * 100

This expression assumes export shares are stored as decimal fractions.

The Tableau implementation uses a country-level FIXED calculation to compare the beginning and end of the period.

**Interpretation:**
- Positive values indicate countries gaining relative export share.
- Negative values indicate countries losing relative export share.
- Results are expressed in percentage points, not percentage growth.

The export share denominator is the total reported beer exports of the EU-27 countries included in the dataset.

## Dashboard 02 — Geographic Trade Dynamics

**Purpose:** Identify geographic differences in export performance and compare the evolution of intra-EU and extra-EU trade.

### Worksheet 3 — EU Export Share Map

**Visualization:** Geographic map.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_kpis`.

**Configuration:**
- Geographic dimension: Country
- Color: Export share change (pp)
- Comparison: 2019–2025
- Geographic coverage: EU-27

The map provides a geographic representation of countries gaining or losing relative export share.

It complements the ranking visualization in Dashboard 01 by making geographic patterns easier to identify.

**Interpretation:**

Color intensity represents the magnitude and direction of export share changes.

This map displays relative changes in export position rather than absolute export volumes.

### Worksheet 4 — Intra-EU vs Extra-EU Export Growth

**Visualization:** Comparative bar chart.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_integrated`.

**Configuration:**
- Countries: Czechia, Spain and Ireland
- Trade flow: `EXPORT`
- Partner groups:
  - `INT_EU27_2020`
  - `EXT_EU27_2020`
- Comparison: 2019 versus 2025
- Measure: Export volume growth (%)

**Calculated fields:**

#### Export Volume 2019 FIXED

Country-partner export volume for 2019.

#### Export Volume 2025 FIXED

Country-partner export volume for 2025.

#### Export Growth (%)

Conceptually:

    ((Export Volume 2025 FIXED /
      Export Volume 2019 FIXED) - 1) * 100

The Tableau calculations use FIXED level-of-detail expressions at the country and partner-group levels.

**Purpose of the calculation:**

FIXED expressions preserve the intended comparison between 2019 and 2025 for each country and trade-partner group.

The visualization highlights that export growth can differ substantially between intra-EU and extra-EU destinations.

**Important:** Growth percentages should not be interpreted as changes in market share or absolute trade volumes.

## Dashboard 03 — Export Intensity & Economic Context

**Purpose:** Extend the trade analysis by incorporating demographic and economic indicators from the World Bank.

### Worksheet 5 — Beer Exports per Capita — 2025

**Visualization:** Horizontal bar chart.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_kpis`.

**Configuration:**
- Rows: Country
- Columns: Average exports per capita (kg/person)
- Year filter: 2025
- Country selection: Top 10
- Sorting: Descending
- Data labels: Two decimal places

**Indicator: Exports Per Capita Kg**

Calculated in the Python pipeline as:

    export_kg / population

Where:
- `export_kg` is the reported beer export volume in kilograms.
- `population` is the World Bank population indicator for the corresponding country and year.

**Purpose:**

This indicator normalizes export volumes by population, making countries of different demographic sizes more comparable.

**Interpretation:**

A higher value indicates a larger reported beer export volume relative to the country's population.

This metric does not represent domestic beer consumption or production, and can be influenced by re-exports and international distribution activities.

### Worksheet 6 — GDP vs Beer Exports per Capita — 2025

**Visualization:** Scatter plot.

**Data source:** `beer_trade_kpis`.

**Configuration:**
- Columns: `AVG(gdp_per_capita_usd)`
- Rows: `AVG(exports_per_capita_kg)`
- Marks: Circle
- Detail: Country
- Year filter: 2025
- Geographic coverage: 27 EU countries
- Tooltips: Country, GDP per capita and exports per capita

**X-axis:** GDP per capita (current USD).

**Y-axis:** Beer exports per capita (kg/person).

**Purpose:**

The scatter plot investigates whether countries with different income levels also display different levels of beer export intensity.

Each observation represents one country in 2025.

The World Bank GDP-per-capita variable provides additional economic context to the Eurostat trade data.

**Interpretation:**

Any visible association should be treated as descriptive.

The chart cannot establish whether GDP per capita causes higher or lower beer exports per capita.

## Key Calculated Fields

The workbook uses the following calculated measures.

| Calculated field | Definition | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Export Share Change (pp) | Export share in 2025 minus export share in 2019, expressed in pp | Relative export position |
| Export Volume 2019 FIXED | Country-partner export volume in 2019 | Growth baseline |
| Export Volume 2025 FIXED | Country-partner export volume in 2025 | Final-period comparison |
| Export Growth (%) | `(Volume 2025 / Volume 2019 - 1) * 100` | Percentage export growth |
| Exports Per Capita Kg | `export_kg / population` | Export intensity per resident |

The formulas above describe the analytical definitions. The corresponding workbook fields should be consulted for the exact Tableau syntax and level-of-detail expressions.

## Filters and Tableau Calculation Logic

The dashboards use different filtering strategies according to the analytical purpose.

### EU Beer Trade Overview
- Covers the period 2019–2025.
- The export share change calculation compares fixed endpoints: 2019 and 2025.

### Geographic Trade Dynamics
- The map compares export share changes between 2019 and 2025.
- The trade growth chart includes only exports.
- The selected partner groups are intra-EU and extra-EU.
- The country comparison focuses on Czechia, Spain and Ireland.

### Export Intensity & Economic Context
- Both worksheets use the year 2025.
- The exports-per-capita ranking shows the ten highest-ranking countries.
- The GDP versus exports-per-capita scatter plot shows all 27 countries.

### Tableau Order of Operations

FIXED level-of-detail calculations are evaluated according to Tableau's order of operations.

Ordinary dimension filters do not necessarily affect FIXED calculations in the same way as context filters.

For this reason, the fixed 2019–2025 comparisons should be preserved when editing filters.

The Top 10 exports-per-capita view should also be checked after filter changes to ensure that the ranking refers to the intended 2025 observations.

## Reproducing and Refreshing the Workbook

### 1. Install dependencies

From the repository root:

    uv sync

### 2. Run the data pipeline

    uv run python run_pipeline.py

This regenerates the cleaned, integrated and KPI datasets used in Tableau.

### 3. Open Tableau

Open:

    tableau/European_Beer_Trade_Dashboard.twbx

The packaged workbook includes the saved worksheets, dashboard layouts and associated data.

### 4. Refresh data connections

If Tableau requests a data source location, reconnect the corresponding source to:

    data/processed/beer_trade_kpis.csv

or:

    data/processed/beer_trade_integrated.csv

Then refresh the data source.

### 5. Verify the dashboards

Confirm that the workbook contains:

**Dashboard 01 — EU Beer Trade Overview**
- EU-27 Beer Export Volume (2019–2025)
- Export Share Winners & Losers

**Dashboard 02 — Geographic Trade Dynamics**
- EU Export Share Map
- Intra-EU vs Extra-EU Export Growth

**Dashboard 03 — Export Intensity & Economic Context**
- Beer Exports per Capita — 2025
- GDP vs Beer Exports per Capita — 2025

### 6. Save as a packaged workbook

Save the final file as a Tableau Packaged Workbook (`.twbx`) to retain the workbook and associated data in a portable format.

## Data Quality and Limitations

The following considerations apply when interpreting the dashboard results.

1. **Data revisions:** Recent trade observations, including 2025, may be revised by the reporting sources.
2. **Reporting differences:** Eurostat statistics may reflect methodological differences or reporting adjustments.
3. **Re-exports:** Export volumes may include goods re-exported through trading and distribution hubs.
4. **Interpretation of per-capita values:** Exports per capita are not equivalent to consumption or production per capita.
5. **Macroeconomic comparison:** GDP per capita provides economic context but does not establish causality.
6. **Data coverage:** Results are limited to the selected EU-27 countries, years and trade classifications.
7. **Trade-flow aggregation:** Summing member-country exports does not produce a unique measure of exports from the EU as a single customs territory.

The Python pipeline includes data validation, integration checks and automated tests, but these checks do not independently verify the accuracy of the underlying official statistics.

For further details, see:

- `docs/data_sources.md`
- `docs/quality_note.md`
